# The Everyday Life of Da Feiyu

A Halloween dress-up interactive web app starring the chibi Da Feiyu whale maid mascot. Built for the Kiroween 2026 hackathon (Costume Contest).

Tap to mix and match spooky costumes, hats and accessories on Da Feiyu, then export the final look as a PNG image.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No backend, no login, no build step.

## Features

- Dress-up picker in four groups: outfits, hats, accessories, backgrounds
- 4 curated preset looks, one-tap random, one-tap reset to the default maid outfit
- 6 Da Feiyu-style Halloween lines, switchable
- Outfit state saved in localStorage, survives refresh
- Export the final look as a 1028x1494 PNG (with title and line strip)

## Character

The mascot art is an original character design by the author (chibi maid version of Da Feiyu). Costume pieces are hand-drawn SVG overlay layers; the face, hair and tail of the base art are untouched.
