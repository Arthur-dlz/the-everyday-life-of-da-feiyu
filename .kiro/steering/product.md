# Product

The Everyday Life of Da Feiyu is a single-page Halloween dress-up web app
starring the chibi Da Feiyu whale maid mascot.

- The user picks outfits, hats, accessories and backgrounds for Da Feiyu.
- One-tap random look, one-tap reset to the default maid outfit.
- The final look exports as a PNG image with a title and a Da Feiyu-style
  Halloween line.
- No backend, no login. Outfit state is stored in localStorage.

## Character rules

- Only the author's own chibi maid version of Da Feiyu is used.
- Costume pieces are overlay layers only. Never redraw or alter the face,
  hair, apron or tail of the base art.
- All content is fictional example data. No real company cases, internal
  data or customer information.
